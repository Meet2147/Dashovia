Nakshatra

Privacy Policy

Nakshatra for iPhone · Effective 4 November 2026

In short: Your birth charts are calculated and saved on your iPhone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. When you ask the AI astrologer a question, the chart summary and your question are sent to our server and to Anthropic to write the answer. We don't store them. We keep only a random ID and your question balance.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Birth details: names, gender, relation, birth date, time and place for every chart you add.
  • Your charts and readings: kundli, divisional charts, dashas, panchang, matching results, your past questions and answers, and daily readings.

All chart calculations happen on your iPhone. This data is saved in the app's private storage and is not uploaded unless you ask a question (see below).

When you ask a question

Answers are written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. When you ask a question, request your daily reading, or ask for a compatibility reading, the app sends:

  • a text summary of the relevant chart(s): the name, gender and relation you entered, birth date, time, place and coordinates, planet positions, houses, yogas and dashas;
  • today's transits and panchang;
  • your question and the earlier messages in that conversation.

This goes to our server (hosted on Cloudflare), which passes it to Anthropic and streams the answer back. Our server does not store or log any of it. Request logging is switched off. Anthropic processes the request under its commercial terms. It does not use this data to train its models, and may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

To avoid sending a real name, use a nickname when you add a chart.

What our server keeps

  • A random user ID created on your iPhone. It isn't linked to your name, Apple ID, email or phone number.
  • Your question balance and the date of your last free daily reading.
  • App Store transaction IDs for packs you've bought, so each purchase is credited once.
  • An App Attest key: a public key from Apple that proves requests come from the genuine app on a real iPhone.

Purchases

Question packs are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Our server checks Apple's signature on each purchase before adding questions to your balance.

What we don't do

  • No sign-in or account.
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Delete a chart by swiping it in the Profiles tab, delete a conversation by long-pressing it in Ask, or delete the app to remove everything on your iPhone. To delete your balance record from our server, email us the support code shown in Profiles › Questions and we'll remove it within 30 days. Unused questions are lost when the record is deleted.

Guidance, not advice

Nakshatra is for guidance and reflection. It is not a substitute for medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.

Children

Nakshatra is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy or need support? Email contact@dashovia.app or see the support page.
