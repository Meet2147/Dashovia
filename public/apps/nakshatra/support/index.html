Nakshatra for iPhone

Need a hand? Email contact@dashovia.app and we'll reply within two working days. If it's about questions or a purchase, include the support code from Profiles › Questions.

Your chart

How accurate is it?

Planet positions are calculated on your iPhone and checked against Swiss Ephemeris, the reference most astrology software uses, to within about 20 arcseconds. Nakshatra uses the sidereal zodiac with Lahiri ayanamsa, whole-sign houses, mean Rahu/Ketu and Vimshottari dasha.

My lagna looks wrong

The lagna changes about every two hours, so a few minutes can matter near a sign boundary. Check the birth time (from the birth certificate, if you can) and the birth place, then edit the chart by swiping it in Profiles.

North or South Indian chart?

Switch between them at the top of the Kundli tab. Use the chart picker to see D9 (Navamsa), D10 (Dasamsa) and other divisional charts.

Questions and packs

What's free?

Kundli, divisional charts, dashas, yogas, panchang, transits and Guna Milan are always free. New users get 3 free questions and free daily readings for 14 days. Any question pack keeps daily readings on.

How do packs work?

Packs of 5, 20 or 50 questions are one-time purchases and never expire. Each question, follow-up or compatibility reading uses one. If the astrologer can't answer, the question is refunded automatically.

I bought a pack but my balance didn't change

Close and reopen the app while online. Purchases are added once our server confirms them with Apple. If it still hasn't appeared, email us with your support code. For refunds, Apple handles requests at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do my questions move to a new phone?

Your balance is tied to this iPhone. If you're switching phones, email us with the support codes from both and we'll move it.

Privacy

Charts stay on your iPhone. Questions are answered by Anthropic's Claude and are not stored by us. Read the privacy policy.
