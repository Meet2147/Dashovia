Tapped In

Privacy Policy

Tapped In for iPhone · Effective 26 November 2026

In short: Tapped In has no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you save stays on your iPhone. We (Dashovia) can't see any of it, and we don't collect, sell or share any personal data.

What the app stores

  • Your things: the names and icons you give them (for example "house keys") and any notes.
  • Spots: where you left each thing (for example "key bowl, hallway") and when.
  • Map pins: if you allow location, the position of your phone and the area name at the moment you tap a sticker or add a thing.

All of this is saved in a database inside the app on your iPhone. It is not uploaded anywhere.

NFC stickers

When you link a sticker, Tapped In writes a short link to it (like tappedin://i/<id>). The link contains a random ID, not your name or the thing's name. Anyone who scans the sticker with another phone only sees that ID.

Location

Tapped In asks for location only while you're using the app, and only checks it when you tap a sticker or add a thing. It never tracks you in the background. The coordinates are turned into an area name (like "Ghatkopar West, Mumbai") using Apple's geocoding service, which receives the coordinates under Apple's privacy policy. You can turn location off at any time in iPhone Settings › Tapped In; the app keeps working, just without map pins.

Links to Amazon

The sticker guide has a button that opens Amazon to buy NFC stickers. Once you're on Amazon, its own privacy policy applies. Tapped In doesn't send Amazon any of your data.

Purchases

Tapped In Pro is a one-time purchase sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether your Apple ID owns Pro so it can unlock it.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up or account with us.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Delete a thing or a spot from inside the app, or delete the app to erase everything. Since we hold no copy of your data, there's nothing more for us to delete.

Children

Tapped In is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. We collect no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy or need support? Email contact@dashovia.app or see the support page.
