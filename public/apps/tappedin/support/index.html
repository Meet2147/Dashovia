Support

Tapped In for iPhone

Need help? Email contact@dashovia.app with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within a day or two.

Questions

Which stickers do I need?

Any NTAG213 or NTAG215 NFC sticker works. For keys or anything metal, use an anti-metal sticker, because normal stickers don't read on bare metal. Avoid "MIFARE Classic" tags; iPhones can't write to them. The sticker guide in the app (bag icon) links to a pack that works.

How do I link a sticker?

Add a thing with +, keep link a sticker now on, and hold the top of your iPhone (near the camera) against the sticker until it buzzes. You can also open a thing and tap re-link sticker.

The phone won't read the sticker

  • Touch the sticker with the top edge of the iPhone, not the middle. Small stickers need to be almost touching.
  • Take the phone out of thick or metal cases.
  • On metal (keys, belt buckle), use an anti-metal sticker.
  • If it says the sticker is read-only, it was locked by the seller. Use a fresh one.

Does it work on iPad?

No. iPads don't have NFC, so Tapped In is iPhone-only (iPhone 7 or newer).

Can it track my stuff live like an AirTag?

No. NFC stickers have no battery and don't send any signal. Tapped In remembers where you last tapped each thing. For live tracking, put an AirTag on it and use Apple's Find My.

Why is the map pin on my building and not my room?

GPS is accurate to about 5–20 metres, so indoors every pin lands on your building. That's why you also pick a spot like "key bowl". The map helps most when you leave something outside home.

There's no map pin on my check-in

Check iPhone Settings › Tapped In › Location is set to While Using the App. Deep inside buildings the phone may also take a while to find you.

What's free, and what does Pro add?

Free includes up to 3 things, with stickers, spots, search, history and the sus list. Tapped In Pro is a one-time purchase (no subscription) that unlocks unlimited things and the map with walking directions.

I bought Pro but it's locked

Open the sticker guide (bag icon) and tap restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my data private?

Yes. There's no account and no server. Everything stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
