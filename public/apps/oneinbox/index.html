FAQ

Questions, answered

What do I need?

A Mac running macOS 15 Sequoia or later, Apple silicon or Intel.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Download OneInbox and you get every feature, including multiple accounts, free for 3 days. No card, no sign-up.

What happens when the trial ends?

OneInbox asks you to pick a plan. Your connected apps, accounts and settings are kept, so everything comes right back when you paste your license key.

How do I unlock it after paying?

Right after checkout you get an email from Dodo Payments with your license key. Open OneInbox → Settings → License, paste the key, and you're done. One key works on up to 3 of your Macs.

How does iMessage work?

OneInbox reads your Messages history on your Mac and sends replies through the Messages app. macOS asks you once to allow Full Disk Access. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Is there any setup for Telegram or Slack?

They work right away. For native chats, Telegram and Slack each need a one-time setup, and OneInbox walks you through it.

Can I use more than one WhatsApp, Instagram or Slack account?

Yes. Early-access buyers get 2 accounts per app for life. Pro unlocks unlimited accounts per app. Every account is signed in separately, so they never mix.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

You keep OneInbox. Accounts beyond your plan pause and keep their sign-in, so they pick up where they left off if you come back.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not right now. OneInbox is made for the Mac.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Email us within 14 days of purchase and we'll refund you in full. See the refund policy.
