Questions

What do I need?

A Mac running macOS 15 Sequoia or later, Apple silicon or Intel.

How do I get the app after paying?

Right after checkout you get an email from Dodo Payments with a download link for the OneInbox installer (a .dmg file). You can get fresh links any time from the customer portal linked in that email.

How does iMessage work?

OneInbox reads your Messages history on your Mac and sends replies through the Messages app. macOS asks you once to allow Full Disk Access. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How do WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and Discord work?

OneInbox runs each service's official web app inside its own tab and collects new-message notifications into your inbox. You sign in once, the same way you would in a browser.

Is there any setup for Telegram or Slack?

Only if you want them native. They work as web apps out of the box. For native chats, Telegram needs a free API key from Telegram, and Slack needs a small private Slack app. OneInbox walks you through each step.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not right now. OneInbox is made for the Mac.
