Refund Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

We want you to love OneInbox. If you don't, we'll give you your money back.

14-day refunds

Within 14 days of your purchase, email contact@dashovia.app with the email address you bought with, and we'll refund you in full. No questions asked.

How refunds work

  • Refunds are issued by Dodo Payments to your original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank.
  • After a refund, your license key stops working and OneInbox returns to its trial-ended screen. Please delete the app.
  • The one-time Early access purchase has nothing to cancel.
  • OneInbox Pro is a subscription. You can cancel any time from the customer portal linked in your receipt, and it stays active until the end of the period you paid for. The 14-day refund covers your first Pro payment.

After 14 days

We'll still look at every request, for example if the app can't run on your Mac. Just get in touch.
