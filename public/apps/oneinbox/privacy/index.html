OneInbox

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

OneInbox is a Mac app made by Dashovia. This policy explains what happens to your information when you buy and use it.

The short version

OneInbox has no servers and no accounts. Your messages, contacts and sign-ins stay on your Mac. We don't collect analytics, and we never see, store or sell your messages.

Inside the app

  • Messages are fetched directly from each service (Apple Messages on your Mac, Telegram, Slack, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Discord) and shown on your Mac. They are not sent to Dashovia.
  • iMessage history is read locally from your Mac's Messages database, read-only, after you grant Full Disk Access. Replies are sent through the Messages app.
  • Contacts are read locally, if you allow it, only to show names instead of phone numbers.
  • Sign-ins and keys (Telegram, Slack) are stored in your Mac's Keychain. Telegram's local data is encrypted on disk.
  • Web apps (WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Discord) run inside OneInbox like in a browser. Their cookies stay on your Mac, and their own privacy policies apply.

Each service you connect has its own terms and privacy policy, and your use of it is governed by them.

When you buy

Purchases are processed by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record. Dodo collects your name, email, billing details and payment information to process the order, calculate tax, deliver the download and handle refunds. We receive your name, email and order details so we can support you. We don't receive your full card details.

Email

We only email you about your purchase, important updates to OneInbox, or replies to your support requests.

Your choices

You can remove all OneInbox data by deleting the app and its folder at ~/Library/Application Support/OneInbox, and signing out of connected services in the app. To ask about or delete your purchase records, email contact@dashovia.app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
