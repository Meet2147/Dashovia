Privacy Policy

AudioLift for iPhone and iPad · Effective 16 October 2026

In short: AudioLift extracts audio from your videos entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. Your videos and the audio you make are never uploaded. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

Your videos

AudioLift only sees the videos you choose to open, whether you pick them from your photo library, the Files app or drag them in. The app uses Apple's photo picker, so it never gets access to the rest of your library.

Audio extraction, trimming and format conversion all happen on your device. Videos are copied to the app's temporary storage while they are processed and are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Your audio library

The audio files you create are saved in AudioLift's own storage on your device, along with a list of your extractions. They also appear in the Files app under On My iPhone (or iPad) › AudioLift, so you can manage them there. They are not uploaded or backed up by us. Deleting a file in the app, or deleting the app, removes them.

When you share or export a file, it goes only where you send it, using Apple's share sheet or file exporter.

Settings and free usage

Your preferred audio format and the count of free extractions you have used today are stored on your device. They never leave it.

Purchases

AudioLift Premium subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple on your device to unlock Premium.

Subscriptions are governed by Apple's standard Terms of Use (EULA). They renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and you can manage or cancel them in your Apple Account settings.

What we don't do

  • No sign-in or account.
  • No uploading of your videos or audio.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
