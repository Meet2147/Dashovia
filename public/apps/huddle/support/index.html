Support

Huddle for iPhone

Need help? Email contact@dashovia.app with your device model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within a day or two.

Questions

How do I add friends?

Make a habit with With friends turned on, or open a habit and tap Invite. Send the link (or show the QR code) to your friends. When they tap it, Huddle opens and they join. There are no usernames to search.

My friend tapped the link but it opened in Safari

Make sure they've installed Huddle and are signed in to iCloud. They can also copy the link and paste it into the got a link? box on the Squads tab.

Do my friends and I need to be near each other?

No. Huddle syncs through iCloud over the internet, so you can be anywhere.

It says I'm not signed in to iCloud

Open iPhone Settings › [your name] and sign in, then make sure iCloud Drive is on. Until then your habits still work on your phone, but you can't share them.

What's a poke?

If a friend in your squad hasn't checked in today, tap Poke 👉 next to their name. They get a notification.

What's free, and what does Pro add?

Free includes 3 habits you start, 1 of them shared, with up to 5 people each. Joining a friend's habit is always free. Pro unlocks unlimited habits and squads, up to 50 people per squad, 2 streak freezes a month and a 6-month history. Choose yearly (with a 1-week free trial), monthly or a one-time Lifetime unlock.

Free trial, cancelling and refunds

  • Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you won't be charged.
  • Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
  • Changed phones or reinstalled? Tap Restore on the paywall.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?

Open Me › Delete my account. This erases your habits from your phone and your iCloud, removes you from squads, and deletes squads you started for everyone in them. If you're subscribed, cancel the subscription in Settings too, since Apple bills it separately.

Is my data private?

Yes. There's no account with us and no server. Your habits live on your phone and in your own iCloud. See the privacy policy.
