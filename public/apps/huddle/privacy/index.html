Privacy Policy

Huddle for iPhone · Effective 11 November 2026

In short: Huddle has no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your habits are saved on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We (Dashovia) can't see any of it, and we don't collect, sell or share any personal data.

What the app stores

  • Your habits: names, icons, weekly goals and squad names.
  • Your check-ins: which days you checked in, and any streak freezes you used.
  • Your display name and colour, which you type into the app. It doesn't have to be your real name.
  • Pokes you send to or receive from friends.

This is saved in a database inside the app on your device.

iCloud

If you choose Lock in with iCloud, Huddle backs up your habits to your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. This storage belongs to your Apple ID. It counts toward your iCloud storage, and we have no access to it. If you choose demo mode, nothing leaves your phone.

Sharing with friends

When you share a habit, Huddle creates an iCloud share link. Anyone who opens that link and joins can see the habit, the display names of people in it, their check-ins and pokes. They do not see your Apple ID, email address or phone number. Sharing happens through Apple's iCloud, not through any server of ours.

  • The owner of a squad can remove people or stop sharing at any time. Stopping sharing cuts off everyone else.
  • Members can leave a squad at any time.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Huddle shows alerts on your device, for example when a friend pokes you. These are created on your device from data that arrives through iCloud. We don't run a notification server.

Purchases

Huddle Pro subscriptions and the Lifetime unlock are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your Apple ID has an active purchase so it can unlock Pro.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up or account with us.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Open Me › Delete my account in the app. Huddle deletes every habit you started from your iCloud (which also removes those squads for the people in them), removes you from squads you joined, and erases everything stored on your device. You can also delete single habits or leave squads at any time. Since we hold no copy of your data, there's nothing more for us to delete.

Children

Huddle is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. We collect no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy or need support? Email contact@dashovia.app or see the support page.
