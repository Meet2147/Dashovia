Fitcheck

Privacy Policy

Fitcheck for iPhone · Effective 3 November 2026

In short: Fitcheck has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your clothing photos, outfits, occasions and settings are saved only on your iPhone. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

What the app stores

  • Your closet: photos of your clothes, with the name, type, colour and dress level you set, plus wear counts and laundry status.
  • Your plans: the occasions you add (for example a presentation or the gym), your office days and dress code, and the outfits picked for each day.
  • Imported pieces: product links you share or paste in, and the shop's address kept on the piece.

All of this is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. It is not uploaded to any server of ours. The share extension hands links to the app through a private storage area on your device (an App Group), so nothing leaves your phone that way either.

Photos, camera and Face ID

Fitcheck uses the camera and photo library only when you choose to add a piece or scan an order screenshot. Background removal, colour detection, type guessing and reading text from screenshots all run on your iPhone using Apple's built-in Vision framework. Images are never uploaded. If you turn on the app lock, Face ID or your passcode is checked by iOS; the app never sees your face data.

Product links

Fitcheck reads a product's name from the link address itself. If you share a short link (such as myntr.it), the app asks that address once, directly from your iPhone, where it leads. That request goes straight to the shop and is covered by the shop's own privacy policy. It never passes through us.

Shopping ideas (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Shopping ideas, Fitcheck may suggest a piece your closet is missing for an occasion and show links to shops' search pages, for example Myntra, AJIO or Amazon. Tapping a link opens the shop in your browser. Some links may be affiliate links, which means we may earn a small commission if you buy something; your price doesn't change. Fitcheck sends nothing about you or your closet to the shop or to us. The search words come from the suggestion only.

Purchases

Fitcheck Pro subscriptions and the Lifetime unlock are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your Apple ID has an active purchase so it can unlock Pro.

Notifications

With your permission, Fitcheck schedules local notifications on your iPhone with tomorrow's outfit. They are created on your device; we don't run a push server and receive no data about them.

What we don't do

  • No sign-in or account.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Erase everything in Fitcheck, or delete the app. Both remove its data from your iPhone. We hold no copy, so there's nothing more for us to delete.

Children

Fitcheck is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy or need support? Email contact@dashovia.app or see the support page.
