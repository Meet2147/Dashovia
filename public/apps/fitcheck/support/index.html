Getting started

How do I add clothes?

Open the Closet tab and tap Snap to take a photo, or Photos to pick several at once. Fitcheck removes the background and guesses the colour and type; check them and save. Start with what you wear most: a few tops, two bottoms and a pair of shoes is enough for the first suggestions.

How does Fitcheck pick an outfit?

It looks at your office days and dress code, any occasions you've added (a presentation steps it up to business, a wedding to formal, the gym packs a gym bag), what you wore recently and what's in the laundry, and which colours go together. Tap Shuffle for another option or Lock it in to keep it.

When do I get the reminder?

Every evening at the time you set in Settings › Reminders (9 pm by default), with tomorrow's outfit. Make sure notifications for Fitcheck are allowed in the iPhone Settings app.

Importing from shops

Can I add things I bought online?

Yes. In Myntra, AJIO, Amazon or another shop, open the product, tap Share and choose Fitcheck. It's ready to add the next time you open the app. You can also paste a product link, or scan a screenshot of your My Orders page from Closet › Import. Everything is read on your iPhone.

Why does an imported piece show an outline instead of a photo?

Links don't include your own photo. Open the piece and tap Add photo any time.

Fitcheck Pro

The free plan holds 25 pieces and plans 7 days ahead. Pro unlocks an unlimited closet, planning weeks ahead and the Face ID lock. It's available yearly or monthly (both with a 7-day free trial) or as a one-time Lifetime purchase.

How do I restore a purchase?

Settings › Fitcheck Pro › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel?

Settings › Fitcheck Pro › Manage subscription, or in the iPhone Settings app › your name › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
