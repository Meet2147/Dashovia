Questions

What are the four windows?

Sortd is the Eisenhower matrix. do it now is urgent and important, plan it is important but not urgent, pass it is urgent but not important (delegate it), and the recycle bin is neither. Drag a task between windows when its priority changes, or use "move to" from its menu.

How does quick add work?

Type a task like a text. !! means urgent and important, ! or "important" means important, "urgent" means urgent, and "meh" or "later" sends it to the recycle bin. Add a day (today, tmrw, fri), a time (7am, 18:30), a colour (#red) or a repeat (every week). Example: pay rent tmrw 6pm !! #red every month.

How do repeating tasks work?

When you tick a repeating task, Sortd marks it done and creates the next one with the next due date. "Every weekday" means Monday to Friday.

Where are the widgets?

On iPhone or iPad, press and hold the Home Screen, tap the + button and search for Sortd: the matrix, one window, year progress and streak widgets, plus Lock Screen widgets. On Mac, right-click the desktop › Edit Widgets. There's also a "quick add" control for Control Center and the Action button.

My tasks aren't on my other device

Sortd syncs through iCloud. Make sure both devices use the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on, and check Sortd › control panel › icloud sync. Changes usually appear within a minute. On Android or Windows, sign in with the same Apple ID in Sortd's settings.

Reminders aren't showing up

Allow notifications for Sortd in Settings › Notifications. A reminder is only set when a task has a due date and "remind me" is ticked in its properties.

Can I bring in my Apple Reminders?

Yes: control panel › apple apps › import reminders. Imported tasks land in a window based on their priority, and ticking them in Sortd ticks them in Reminders too.

What's free and what's Pro?

Free gives you up to 20 open tasks, the full matrix, quick add, reminders and all widgets. Sortd Pro adds unlimited tasks, repeating tasks, subtasks, the focus timer, the calendar view and insights. Pro is $3.99 a month, $19.99 a year (with a 7-day free trial), or $39.99 once for lifetime.

How do I cancel or restore a purchase?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Sortd. On a new device, open Sortd › control panel › sortd pro › restore.

Is it private?

Yes. No accounts, no servers, no tracking. See the privacy policy.
