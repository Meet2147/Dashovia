Privacy Policy

Sortd: Eisenhower Matrix for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and Windows · Effective 21 November 2026

In short: Sortd has no accounts of ours, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we run no servers. Your tasks are saved on your device and in your own private iCloud. We never see them, and we do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

Your tasks

Task titles, notes, due dates, reminders, colour tags, subtasks, repeat rules and focus minutes are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, they're also kept in your private iCloud database so they sync between your devices. That storage belongs to your Apple account; we can't read it.

Android and Windows

On Android and Windows, Sortd syncs with the same private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit web service. You sign in on Apple's own sign-in page; Sortd only receives a sign-in token from Apple, which is kept on your device so it can sync. We never see your Apple ID password or your tasks. Signing out in Sortd's settings removes the token.

Apple Reminders and Calendar

If you choose to, Sortd can import your Apple Reminders and show your Apple Calendar events next to your tasks. This uses Apple's on-device access and only happens after you allow it in the system prompt. Imported reminders become Sortd tasks on your device; ticking one off in Sortd ticks it off in Reminders too. Calendar events are only read and displayed, never copied or uploaded. You can turn access off any time in Settings › Privacy & Security.

Notifications and focus timer

Reminders for due tasks and the focus timer are local notifications scheduled on your device. The focus timer can show a Live Activity on your Lock Screen. We don't run a push server.

Widgets and Siri

Widgets read your tasks from Sortd's storage on the same device. Siri and Shortcuts actions you set up run on your device and use the same data.

Purchases

Sortd Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store (and by Google Play on Android). We never see your payment details.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up or account with us.
  • No servers of ours: nothing you write is uploaded to us.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Deleting a task removes it from all your devices. Deleting the app removes its data from that device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Sortd.

Children

Sortd is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
