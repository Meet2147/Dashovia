Privacy Policy

Tutorly: Study Any Video for iPhone, iPad and Mac · Effective 19 November 2026

In short: Tutorly has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we run no servers. Your study kits are made on your device and saved on your device and in your own private iCloud. We never see them, and we do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

Your study kits

The videos you add, their notes, chapters, quizzes, flashcards, study plans, streaks and answers are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, they're also kept in your private iCloud database so they sync between your iPhone, iPad and Mac. That storage belongs to your Apple account; we can't read it. A small counter of how many kits you've made is kept in your iCloud key-value storage so the free plan works the same on all your devices.

How a kit is made

When you paste a YouTube link, your device contacts YouTube directly to get the video's title and its captions, or, when captions aren't available, its audio. The audio is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition on your device and deleted right after. Notes, quizzes and flashcards are then written on your device by Apple Intelligence where available, or by Tutorly's built-in engine. Nothing goes through a server of ours.

Because your device talks to YouTube, YouTube receives the request like any visit from your device. Videos play in YouTube's official embedded player, which we load without keeping YouTube cookies on your device. Google's privacy policy applies to YouTube.

If you paste a transcript instead of a link, it is processed the same way, on your device.

Ask the video

Questions you ask about a video are answered on your device from that video's own transcript. They are not sent anywhere.

Purchases

Tutorly Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Notifications

With your permission, Tutorly schedules the daily puzzle as a local notification on your device. Answers you tap in the notification are saved on your device. We don't run a push server.

What we don't do

  • No sign-in or account.
  • No servers of ours: nothing you study is uploaded to us.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Deleting a kit moves it to Recently deleted for 30 days, so you can restore it; after that, or when you choose "Delete for good", it's removed from all your devices. Deleting the app removes its data from that device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Tutorly.

Children

Tutorly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
