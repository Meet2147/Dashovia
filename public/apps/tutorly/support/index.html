Tutorly: Study Any Video for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Need help? Email contact@dashovia.app and we'll get back to you, usually within a day or two.

Questions

How do I make a study kit?

Copy a YouTube link (a lecture, tutorial or crash course), paste it on the Library screen and tap "Make my study kit". Tutorly writes a TL;DR, chapters, key concepts, a quiz and flashcards.

Why does it take a minute?

When YouTube doesn't share a video's captions, Tutorly listens to the audio on your device to write the transcript. That keeps everything private, and takes about a minute for a 5-minute video, longer for long lectures.

It says it couldn't read the video

Some videos are private, age-restricted or blocked. Open the video on YouTube, tap ••• or the description, choose "Show transcript", copy it, then use "No captions? Paste the transcript" in Tutorly.

How do flashcards work?

Tap the card (or "Show answer") to flip it. Swipe right if you knew it, left if you didn't. Cards you know come back later, right before you'd forget them; cards you didn't know come back in the same session.

What's free and what's Pro?

Free gives you 3 study kits, then 1 new kit a week, with unlimited reviews, quizzes, streaks and daily puzzles for every kit you have. Tutorly Pro adds unlimited kits, study plans, Ask the video and weak-spot drills. Pro is $3.99 a month or $24.99 a year (both with a 7-day free trial), or $39.99 once for lifetime.

How do I cancel or restore a purchase?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Tutorly. On a new device, open Tutorly › Settings › Restore purchases.

My kits aren't on my other device

Kits sync through iCloud. Make sure both devices use the same Apple ID and iCloud is on (Settings › your name › iCloud). New kits usually appear within a minute.

I deleted a kit by mistake

Go to Library › Recently deleted (or Settings › Recently deleted) and tap Restore. Kits stay there for 30 days.

I'm not getting the daily puzzle

Allow notifications for Tutorly in Settings › Notifications, and check the puzzle time in Tutorly › Settings. Press and hold the notification to answer it without opening the app.

Is it private?

Yes. Everything is made on your device, and kits sync only through your own iCloud. See the privacy policy.
