Support

Sapience: Book Wisdom for Life for iPhone

Need help? Email contact@dashovia.app and we'll get back to you, usually within a day or two.

Questions

What is a spill?

One spill = one problem. You get a page from a real book that fits it, a short explanation, three small moves and a check-in a few days later. New installs come with 3 free spills.

What's free?

Check-ins, the "still stuck" second angle, and asking something you've already asked (Sapience reuses its earlier answer) don't use a spill. Your first "another book" on each problem is free too.

Are the quotes real?

Yes. Every page comes from a library of 300+ books built into the app, from Chanakya Niti and Vidur Niti to Seneca, the Gita and Atomic Habits. Older public-domain books are quoted word for word; modern books are paraphrased and marked "paraphrased".

Is it private?

Your history lives only on your iPhone. Answers are written by our server, which stores nothing. You can also switch to on-device-only answers in Settings. See the privacy policy.

I bought spills but don't see them

Make sure you're online, then reopen the app; purchases are added as soon as the App Store confirms them. Still missing? Email us with the date of purchase.

I'm not getting check-in reminders

Allow notifications for Sapience in iPhone Settings › Notifications. Check-ins also appear in the app when they're due.

Is this therapy?

No. Sapience shares ideas from books for everyday problems. If you're struggling or in danger, please contact a professional or a crisis line such as Tele-MANAS (14416, India) or 988 (US).
