Privacy Policy

Sapience: Book Wisdom for Life for iPhone · Effective 29 October 2026

In short: Sapience has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your problems, answers and check-ins are saved only on your iPhone. To write an answer, the problem you type is sent to our answer server, which replies and keeps nothing: no logs, no copies. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

Your history

The problems you write, the book pages and plans you receive, the moves you tick and your check-in answers are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. They are not synced to iCloud or to any server of ours. You can delete any problem from your shelf, or everything at once in Settings › delete all my history.

How answers are made

When "smarter cloud answers" is on (the default), Sapience sends the text of your problem, together with a shortlist of book entries from the app's built-in library, over an encrypted connection to our answer server. The server runs on Cloudflare Workers and uses Cloudflare Workers AI to choose the best book page and write your plan, then returns the reply to your phone.

  • The server does not store or log what you send. Request logging is turned off, and the text exists only while your answer is being written.
  • It is never used to train AI models, never linked to your identity, and never shared or sold.
  • Cloudflare processes the request on our behalf as our infrastructure provider. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Prefer nothing to leave your phone? Turn off smarter cloud answers in Settings. Sapience then answers on device, using Apple's on-device AI where available or the book library's own text.

Keeping the answer server safe

To make sure only the genuine Sapience app can use the answer server, the app uses Apple's App Attest: your iPhone creates a key in its secure hardware, and the server stores only that key's public part and a request counter. It contains nothing about you or what you type. The server also limits how many answers each device can request per minute; this uses that key, or briefly your IP address when the app first registers, and keeps no history.

Purchases

Question packs ("spills") are one-time purchases sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Your spill balance is stored on your iPhone.

Notifications

With your permission, Sapience schedules local reminders on your iPhone for check-ins. They are created on your device; we don't run a push server and receive no data about them.

Sharing

When you share a page, Sapience shares an image of the book quote card only, never the problem you wrote.

What we don't do

  • No sign-in or account.
  • No storing of your problems or answers on any server.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Not professional advice

Sapience offers ideas from books for everyday problems. It is not therapy, medical, legal or financial advice. If you write about being in danger or harming yourself, the app shows crisis helplines instead of a book page.

Deleting your data

Deleting Sapience removes all of its data from your iPhone. We hold no copy of your problems or answers, so there's nothing more for us to delete.

Children

Sapience is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
