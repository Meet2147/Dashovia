Common questions

Do I need internet? No. Every station, line, fare and the City Guide are built into the app. Only the map background needs data.

A fare or timing looks wrong. Fares and frequencies are estimates from published tables. Please email us the stations and what you saw, and we'll fix it in the next update.

A new line opened and isn't in the app. We add lines once they carry passengers. Let us know and we'll prioritise it.

The stop alarm didn't go off. Allow notifications for MetroRouter, and location "While Using". Underground, the alarm falls back to a timed reminder, which can be early or late if trains are delayed.
