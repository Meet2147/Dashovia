Privacy Policy

MetroRouter for iPhone · Effective 9 November 2026

In short: MetroRouter has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Every station, line and fare is built into the app, and your home and work stations, recent trips and commutes are saved only on your iPhone. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • Your choices: your city, app language, home and work stations, recent trips and saved commutes.
  • Widget data: a small summary of your next commute, shared with MetroRouter's own widgets through a private storage area on your device (an App Group).

None of this is sent to us or to any server. Route planning, fares and the City Guide work fully offline.

Location

If you allow it, MetroRouter uses your location on your iPhone to find the nearest station and, for the stop alarm, to alert you as you approach your stop. Your location is never stored by us or sent to us.

Voice route search

If you use the voice assistant, MetroRouter uses Apple's speech recognition to turn what you say into station names. Apple may process the audio to do this, under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive your audio or the transcript.

Maps

Route maps are drawn with Apple Maps, which loads map images from Apple.

Notifications

With your permission, MetroRouter schedules notifications on your iPhone for the stop alarm and for "time to leave" commute reminders. They are created on your device; we don't run a push server.

Purchases

MetroRouter Pro is a one-time purchase sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your Apple ID owns Pro.

What we don't do

  • No sign-in or account.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Accuracy

Timings, frequencies and fares are estimates based on published information and can change. Always check station displays and official operator apps before you travel.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it stored. We hold no copy, so there's nothing more for us to delete.

Children

MetroRouter collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Email meet@dashovia.com or see the support page.
