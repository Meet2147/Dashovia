Is my stuff safe? fr?

We guard your drip like it's ours.

Inspected and photographed

Every item is opened, checked and photographed when we pack it. You agree to this when you sign up. It keeps everyone's things safe, and you get a photo record of everything we hold.

Sealed, coded, tracked

Each box gets a tamper seal and a code like DR-0012, scanned in and out of a locked, CCTV-watched storeroom. Nothing sits on the floor, and pest control is regular.

Private by design

No ads, no selling data. Couriers only see your first name, number and drop address. Rider location is shown only during your delivery, then deleted.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where does Drawr work?

Mumbai first (pincodes starting with 400), starting with areas close to our storeroom. More cities after early access.

Do I need the app?

No. You can sign up on the website and manage everything from your private Drawr page. The apps are ready too: get them here. On iPhone it's on the App Store; on Android it's a direct download.

Why do you open my boxes?

To photograph every item for you, and to make sure nothing illegal or dangerous ends up next to other people's things. You agree to this inspection when you create your account. See section 6 of our Terms.

What if something gets damaged?

Each box is covered up to ₹5,000. Report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos and we'll sort it out.

How do I get everything back?

Select all your boxes in the app and request a delivery. Billing stops the day your last box leaves our storeroom (after the 3-month minimum).
