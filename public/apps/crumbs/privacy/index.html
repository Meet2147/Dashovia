Privacy Policy

Crumbs: Lock In & Focus for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch · Effective 20 October 2026

In short: Crumbs is a focus timer that runs on your devices. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. Your sessions and progress stay on your devices and in your own private iCloud, which we can't access. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

Your focus data

Crumbs stores the sessions you create (title, emoji, crumb length and count), the crumbs you tick (time and duration), and your streaks and stats. All of this is saved on your device.

Your preferences

The name you type during onboarding, your goal, your equipped camo and similar settings are also stored on your device.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, your focus data and preferences sync between your iPhone, iPad and Mac through Apple's iCloud, using your private CloudKit database and iCloud key-value storage. This data is tied to your Apple ID and protected by Apple. We, the developer, can't see it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn off iCloud for Crumbs at any time in your device's Settings.

Apple Watch and widgets

A small summary (today's crumbs, your streak and the current timer) is shared between the Crumbs app, its widgets and your paired Apple Watch. It moves only between your own devices.

Notifications

With your permission, Crumbs schedules local notifications on your device, for example when a crumb's timer finishes. They're created on your device. We don't run a push server and receive no data about them.

Purchases

Crumbs Pro subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple on your device to unlock Crumbs Pro.

Subscriptions are governed by Apple's standard Terms of Use (EULA). Monthly and yearly plans start with a 7-day free trial. They renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and you can manage or cancel them in your Apple Account settings.

What we don't do

  • No sign-in or account.
  • No servers of ours receive your data.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Deleting Crumbs removes its data from that device. To remove synced data from iCloud too, open Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage on your iPhone or iPad and delete Crumbs' data. We don't hold a copy, so there's nothing more for us to delete.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
