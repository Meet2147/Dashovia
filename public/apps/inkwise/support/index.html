Support

Inkwise for iPhone

Need help? Email contact@dashovia.app with your device model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within a day or two.

Questions

How do I scan a page?

Tap scan a page. Hold the phone over the page; it finds the edges and crops it for you. You can scan several pages into one note. You can also import photos with the photo button.

It didn't read my handwriting well

  • Use bright, even light and keep the page flat.
  • Let the page fill most of the frame.
  • Dark ink on plain or lightly ruled paper works best.
  • Lines marked in orange are ones the app wasn't sure about. Tap the pencil to fix them.

Which languages does it read?

Handwriting reading works best in English. Printed text works in many more languages.

What's the handwriting score?

A bit of fun: it shows how confident the app was reading your page, from "cursed scroll" to "certified neat".

What's free, and what does Pro add?

Scanning, reading handwriting and search are free and unlimited. Free also includes 5 AI tries. Pro unlocks unlimited tl;dr summaries, flashcards and ask-your-notes. Choose yearly (with a 1-week free trial), monthly or a one-time Lifetime unlock.

Do the AI features need internet?

Yes. Everything else works offline.

Free trial, cancelling and refunds

  • Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you won't be charged.
  • Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
  • Changed phones or reinstalled? Tap Restore purchases on the Pro screen.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my data private?

Yes. There's no account. Notes stay on your phone, and AI requests aren't stored. See the privacy policy.
