Privacy Policy

Inkwise for iPhone · Effective 15 November 2026

In short: Inkwise has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your scans and notes stay on your iPhone. Reading your handwriting happens on the phone. Only when you tap an AI button is that note's text sent to our AI server, used once to write the answer, and never stored or logged.

What the app stores

  • Your scanned pages: the photos you scan or import.
  • The text read from them, plus any edits you make.
  • AI results you asked for: summaries and flashcards, so you can open them again without another request.

All of this is saved in a database inside the app on your device. We (Dashovia) can't see it.

Reading your handwriting

Inkwise turns handwriting into text with Apple's on-device text recognition. Your pages are not uploaded for this. It works with no internet connection.

AI features

When you tap tl;dr, quiz me or ask, the app sends the text of that one note (and your question, for ask) to the Inkwise AI server, which runs on Cloudflare Workers AI. The text is used only to produce that answer and sent straight back to your phone.

  • Note text and questions are not stored, and request logging is turned off.
  • Your photos are never sent, only the text.
  • Nothing is used to train AI models.
  • To stop abuse, the server checks that requests come from the genuine Inkwise app using Apple's App Attest. It keeps a random device key and a daily request count for that key. These contain no note content and no personal information.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only while you scan a page. If you import from Photos, Inkwise can only see the photos you pick.

Purchases

Inkwise Pro subscriptions and the Lifetime unlock are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your Apple ID has an active purchase so it can unlock Pro.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up or account with us.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Delete a note from its menu (••• › Delete note), or long-press it on the home screen. Deleting the app erases everything. Since we hold no copy of your notes, there's nothing more for us to delete.

Children

Inkwise is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. We collect no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy or need support? Email contact@dashovia.app or see the support page.
