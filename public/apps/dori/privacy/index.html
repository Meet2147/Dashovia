Privacy Policy

DORI for iPhone and Android · Effective 14 November 2026

In short: DORI has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and there are no accounts or passwords. A wedding's guest list, replies and photos are kept only so that wedding can use them. Photos are deleted after the wedding. We never sell or share personal data.

Who uses DORI

DORI (Dashovia) sets up each wedding for a couple, the hosts. The hosts get a private host link. Each invited family gets a personal invite code, usually sent on WhatsApp. Nobody can open a wedding without its link or code.

What we store

  • The wedding: the couple's names, the functions with dates, timings, venues and dress notes, set up by DORI and the hosts.
  • The guest list: for each invited family, the name on the invitation, an optional WhatsApp number and relation, which side they are on, how many people are invited and to which functions. The hosts enter this.
  • Replies: how many people from each family are coming to each function, and whether the invite was opened.
  • Photos: photos uploaded by the wedding's photographers through DORI, and photos guests choose to add from their phone.

We don't collect your email address, location, contacts list, device identifiers or any analytics.

Permissions on your phone

  • Contacts (hosts only): when a host taps Pick from Contacts, the phone shows its contact picker. Only the name and number of the person they pick fill in the form, and only what they save is sent to DORI. On Android, the system asks for Contacts access to read that one contact.
  • Camera and Photos: used only when you choose to add a photo to the wedding albums, or to save a wedding photo to your phone.
  • Microphone: used only if you record a voice blessing in the sample wedding. The recording stays on your phone.

Who can see what

  • Guests see the wedding, the functions they're invited to, their own family's replies and the wedding's photo albums.
  • The hosts see the whole guest list and every reply. A bride's-side or groom's-side host link manages only that side's families.
  • The hosts can hide any guest photo. Removing a family from the guest list deletes the photos that family added.
  • DORI's team can see a wedding's data to set it up and support it.

How long we keep it

Wedding photos are kept for 30 days after the last function, then deleted for good. The hosts can ask DORI to keep them longer. The guest list, replies and wedding details stay until the hosts ask us to delete them. Hosts can ask for that at any time by writing to contact@dashovia.app.

Where it's stored

Wedding data is stored on our servers in Singapore (Render and Neon). Photos are stored in Cloudflare R2 in the Asia-Pacific region and are only shown through short-lived private links. Everything travels over HTTPS.

Your choices

If you're a guest and want your family's details or photos removed, ask the hosts, or write to us and we'll do it. Hosts can edit or remove any family at any time.

Children

DORI is not aimed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
