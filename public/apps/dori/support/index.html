Support

DORI for iPhone and Android

Need a hand? Write to contact@dashovia.app with the couple's names and what went wrong. We usually reply within a day, faster in a wedding week.

I'm a guest

  • Where's my code? It's in the WhatsApp message from the couple's family, and looks like KP-7F3KQ2XM. Tapping the link in that message opens your invitation directly.
  • The code doesn't work. Check for a missing letter, or ask the family to send the invite again from DORI.
  • Changing my reply. Open the function's card and change how many of you are coming. The hosts see it straight away.
  • Saving photos. Open the Vault, tap a photo, then Download HD or Share. Photos are deleted 30 days after the last function, so save your favourites before then.

I'm hosting

  • Getting started. DORI sets up your wedding and sends you a private host link. Open it on your phone, or paste it in the app under Hosts.
  • Adding families. Guest list → Add a family, then send their personal invite on WhatsApp.
  • Changing a function. If editing is turned on for your wedding, use the Functions tab. Otherwise write to us and we'll change it.
  • Keeping photos longer. Ask for the extension from the Vault, or write to us.

Try it without a wedding

Enter AARAV-VANYA or RIYA-DHRUV as the invite code to open a sample wedding. Choose Hosts on the first screen to see the hosts' side.
