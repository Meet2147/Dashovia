Cart Later for iPhone

Need help? Email meetjethwa3@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened (plus the product link if it's about a price). We usually reply within a day or two.

Questions

Cart Later isn't in the Share sheet

In the shopping app, tap Share, scroll the row of apps to the end and tap More. Turn on Cart Later and drag it up so it's easy to reach. Shared links land in your cart the next time you open Cart Later.

The price is wrong or missing

Stores change their pages often, so reading a price sometimes fails. Open the item and tap the price to type it yourself, or pull down on the Cart tab to check prices again.

How does "you can buy it on…" work?

Every payday adds your wishlist amount to the jar. Items are funded in the order of your cart, and each one also waits through a 72-hour cool-off. The date shown is when both are done.

Auto impulse check when I open a shopping app

  • Open Shortcuts › Automation › + › App.
  • Pick Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, AJIO or any other shopping app, then choose Is Opened and Run Immediately.
  • Add the action Impulse Check (Cart Later).

What's free, and what does Pro add?

Free includes the full salary plan, 3 cart spots and 3 impulse checks per pay cycle. Pro removes both limits. Choose yearly (with a 7-day free trial), monthly or a one-time Lifetime unlock.

Free trial, cancelling and refunds

  • Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you won't be charged.
  • Manage or cancel in Cart Later › Settings › Manage subscription, or iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
  • Changed phones or reinstalled? Tap Restore on the paywall or in Settings.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my data private?

Yes. There's no account and no bank login, and everything stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

Is this financial advice?

No. Cart Later uses general budgeting rules of thumb. For investment or tax decisions, talk to a registered advisor.
