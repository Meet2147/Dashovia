Privacy Policy

Cart Later for iPhone · Effective 31 October 2026

In short: Cart Later has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your salary, budget plan, cart and spending log are saved only on your iPhone. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

What the app stores

  • Your plan: the take-home pay, payday, fixed costs and budget style you enter.
  • Your cart: product links you share or paste, with the product name, picture link, price and price history the app reads.
  • Your spends and wins: purchases you log, impulse checks you run and money you "dodged".

All of this is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. It is not synced to iCloud or to any server of ours. The share extension hands links to the app through a private storage area on your device (an App Group), so nothing leaves your phone that way either.

Reading product prices

When you add a link, Cart Later opens that product page directly from your iPhone, the same way a browser would, to read its name, picture and price. That request goes straight to the store (for example Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra or AJIO) and is covered by that store's own privacy policy. It never passes through us.

Purchases

Cart Later Pro subscriptions and the Lifetime unlock are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your Apple ID has an active purchase so it can unlock Pro.

Notifications

With your permission, Cart Later schedules local notifications on your iPhone, for example when an item's cool-off ends or your wishlist jar can cover it. They are created on your device; we don't run a push server and receive no data about them.

What we don't do

  • No sign-in or account, and no bank or card connection.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Not financial advice

Cart Later's budget split uses general rules of thumb. It is not financial, investment or tax advice. For that, talk to a registered advisor.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Start over in Cart Later, or delete the app. Both remove its data from your iPhone. We hold no copy, so there's nothing more for us to delete.

Children

Cart Later is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy or need support? Email contact@dashovia.app or see the support page.
