Privacy and security

Short version: ToneOS listens only while you hold the key, turns your voice into text, and throws the recording away. Last updated 25 November 2026.

When ToneOS listens

  • Only while you hold right ⌥ (or right ⌥ + Shift). Let go and the microphone stops.
  • A listening pill is on screen the whole time, so you always know.

What happens to your voice

  • The recording is sent over an encrypted connection to Sarvam AI, which turns it into text.
  • In the beta, it passes through our server on the way. Our server never saves recordings or the text that comes back.
  • Clips are 30 seconds at most; longer speech is sent in pieces.

What stays on your computer

  • Your last 20 dictations, so you can find them again.
  • Your words, names and per-app writing choices.
  • Your sign-in and any Sarvam key you add, in your Mac's Keychain.
  • With Ask Mode, your documents stay with ToneOS Home on your own computer or ToneOS box.

What our server keeps

  • Your email, if you asked for an invite.
  • Your invite code and the names of the computers you signed in on.
  • How many seconds you spoke each day, so the free minutes work.
  • Corrections, only if you turn on “share my corrections”. They help train an open speech model for Indian languages. This is off unless you turn it on.

How the app is protected

  • The Mac app is signed with our Apple Developer ID and checked by Apple (notarized), so macOS knows it hasn't been changed.
  • It runs with Apple's hardened runtime and asks only for the microphone and Accessibility (to type for you).
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only as one-way hashes on our server.

Questions or a security problem?

Email contact@dashovia.app. If you found a security problem, put “security” in the subject line.
