Most of India thinks in two languages. Keyboards only understand one.

We say “kal meeting hai, sab ko bata dena” and then type it out in broken English letters, one slow word at a time. Writing Gujarati or Tamil in its own script is slower still, so most people don't.

Voice typing should have fixed this years ago. But most dictation tools are built for one language at a time, and they trip over the mix of Hindi and English that most of us speak all day.

ToneOS starts from the way people in India actually talk. Hold one key, speak naturally, switch languages mid-sentence, and get the right text in whatever app you're using: Hinglish for family, clean English for work, your own script when you want it.

What we hold to

01

Speak the way you speak

No “clear, slow English” voice. Mix languages, use your words, and ToneOS keeps up.

02

Work where you already work

No new app to write in. ToneOS types into WhatsApp, Gmail, Slack, your editor: anywhere there's a cursor.

03

Your voice stays yours

It listens only while you hold the key. Recordings become text and are thrown away. Your history stays on your computer.
