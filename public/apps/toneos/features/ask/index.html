Things people ask

Forward a bill, a prescription or a photo of your licence once. After that, just ask.

Money

“Any bills pending?” “How much was the Airtel bill?” “What did I spend in November?”

Health

“When is my next dose?” “How is my sugar since March?” “What was off in my last report?”

Papers

“When does my PUC expire?” “What's my licence number?” “When did I buy the fridge?”

Your documents are kept by ToneOS Home on your own computer, not in our cloud. Answers come from your files, so ToneOS says “I don't know” instead of guessing.
