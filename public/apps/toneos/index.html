Questions

Is it free?

Yes, for the first 250 beta users. Later there will be a free plan and a paid one, and beta users keep a discount.

Which computers does it work on?

macOS 14 or newer today. Windows 10 and 11 is coming next in the same beta.

Which key do I hold?

The right Option key on a Mac and the right Alt key on Windows. Add Shift to ask a question instead of typing.

Does it work offline?

Not yet. Transcription needs the internet today. An offline mode for Indian languages is on the way.

Will it work with my company's apps?

It types into any text field: Slack, Teams, Jira, Notion, browsers and terminals. Doing things inside apps by voice, like replying to an email, comes later.
