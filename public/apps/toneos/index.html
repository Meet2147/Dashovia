What it does

Small, everyday help.

01 · BILLS

Forward it, it's sorted

Send a photo or PDF of a bill, receipt, warranty card or prescription. It's read, filed and remembered.

02 · REMINDERS

Never miss a due date

Bills: two days before and on the day. Warranties: a month before they end. Follow-up visits: the day before.

03 · ASK

Ask in plain words

"What's due this month?" "Summarize the standup notes." Any message becomes a task, and the answer comes back.

04 · MEMORY

It remembers

Tell it "remember that…" once. Every later task can use it. You can see and delete what it remembers.

05 · VISION

It can see

Reads the text in photos, turns a receipt into a record, and finds photos by what's in them.

06 · FAMILY

One box for the home

Pair your phone with a one-time code, then invite family. Strangers and group chats are ignored.

Privacy

Your life stays at home.

  • Your documents, memory and reminders live on your box, not in our cloud. We don't have one.
  • Local models by default. Claude is opt-in, for small Pis or harder questions.
  • A forwarded document is only read, never obeyed, so a sneaky PDF can't instruct your box.
  • Account numbers are kept to their last four digits. Personal data is redacted by rules, not by an AI.
  • Nothing on your home network is opened up; the box only connects out.
  • Telegram carries your messages, so photos you send pass through Telegram on the way.
Questions

Good questions.

Can I get it now?

ToneOS is in early access. Email us and we'll send you the image and setup steps.

Do I need a Raspberry Pi?

No. It also runs on a Mac or Linux machine. A Pi is just a cheap, quiet box that can stay on all the time.

What can I send it?

Photos or PDFs of bills, receipts, warranty cards and prescriptions, plus any question in plain words. Voice notes aren't supported yet.

Does it pay my bills?

No. It reminds you and keeps track. It never moves money.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. On a Pi 4 or 5 it runs local models on the box. If you choose Claude, those API calls are billed to your own Anthropic account.
