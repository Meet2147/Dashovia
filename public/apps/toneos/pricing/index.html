Pricing questions

How do I get the beta?

Download ToneOS for Mac or Windows, open it and type your email. That's it, while the 250 beta seats last.

What happens after 20 minutes?

ToneOS tells you you've used today's minutes. They come back at midnight India time. Twenty minutes is about 3,000 spoken words.

Will I have to pay when the beta ends?

Only if you want Pro. We'll tell you the price well before the beta ends, and nothing is charged without you choosing a plan.

Can my office use it?

Yes. Email contact@dashovia.app and tell us how many people.
