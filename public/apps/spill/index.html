Questions

The fine print, in plain words.

When does it launch?

Soon. Join the waitlist and you'll get one email the day it's on the App Store.

Is it on Android?

iPhone first. If you're on Android, join anyway and we'll know you're waiting.

Where does the product data come from?

Open Beauty Facts, a free database anyone can add to, plus ingredient lists Spill users snap and choose to share. For anything missing, you can snap the label yourself.

Do my photos get uploaded?

No. Label photos are read on your iPhone. If you choose to share a list, only the barcode, product name and ingredient text are sent, so others can scan it too.

Is this medical advice?

No. Spill explains ingredients in plain words. For skin conditions or pregnancy questions, ask a dermatologist or your doctor.

What do you do with my email?

We send you one email when Spill launches. We don't share or sell it, and you can ask us to delete it at contact@dashovia.app. Details in the privacy policy.
