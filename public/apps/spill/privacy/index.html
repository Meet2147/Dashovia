Privacy Policy

Spill for iPhone and the Spill waitlist · Effective 7 November 2026

In short: There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Label photos are read on your iPhone and never uploaded. When you scan a barcode, the barcode is looked up in public databases. If you choose to share an ingredient list, only the barcode, product name and ingredient text are sent. If you join the waitlist, we keep your email only to tell you when Spill launches.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your skin settings: sensitive, breakout-prone, dry, pregnant, and your ick list.
  • Your history and shelf: products you've scanned and the ones you've added to your routine.
  • Label photos: text is read from the photo on your iPhone with Apple's on-device text recognition. The photo is not uploaded or saved by Spill.

When you scan a barcode

The barcode number is sent to look the product up in:

  • Open Beauty Facts, a free public database;
  • Spill's shared ingredient list (our server, hosted on Cloudflare);
  • if neither knows the product, UPCitemdb, to find its name and photo.

Only the barcode is sent. These services see your IP address, as any website would.

When you share an ingredient list

After you snap a label for a product with a barcode, you can choose "Share with everyone" (it's on by default and you can switch it off). If you do, we store:

  • the barcode, the product name and brand you entered, and the ingredient text;
  • a one-way hash of your IP address, used only to limit spam and to count reports. We can't turn it back into your IP.

Shared lists are public: anyone who scans that barcode in Spill sees them. Don't put personal information in the product name.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist on this website, we store your email address, which page you signed up from, the time, and a one-way hash of your IP address (to stop spam). We use your email to send you a message when Spill launches, and nothing else. We don't sell or share it. To be removed, email contact@dashovia.app.

Purchases

Spill Pro is bought through Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app whether you have an active subscription.

Children

Spill is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions or deletion requests: contact@dashovia.app.

Spill is made by Dashovia. Spill explains ingredients in plain words and isn't medical advice.
