Support

Chainz for iPhone

Need help? Email contact@dashovia.app with your device model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within a day or two.

Questions

How do I play with a friend on their own phone?

Tap 1v1 Online › Create a room. Tap Send invite to send the link, or just tell them the 5-character code. Your friend taps the link, or opens Chainz and types the code under Join. You can be anywhere; you both just need internet.

How does the game work?

You each secretly write a chain where every word links to the one before it, like PUMPKIN → PATCH → WORK → OUT. Your opponent sees only your start word and the first letter of each word. Race to crack each other's chain; first to finish wins.

My friend's invite opened in Safari

Tap Open Chainz on that page. If it still doesn't open, open Chainz yourself, tap 1v1 Online and type the room code under Join.

It says "That room doesn't exist"

Rooms delete themselves after 3 hours without activity. Ask your friend to create a new one.

My connection dropped mid-race

Chainz reconnects by itself and puts you back in the same race with your progress. If it can't, open the room code again.

What's free, and what does Pro add?

Free lets you start 3 duels a day, with chains up to 8 words and 1 hint per duel. Joining a friend's room is always free. Pro unlocks unlimited duels, 10 and 12 word chains and 3 hints per duel. Choose yearly (with a 3-day free trial), monthly or a one-time Lifetime unlock.

Free trial, cancelling and refunds

  • Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you won't be charged.
  • Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
  • Changed phones or reinstalled? Tap Restore on the Pro screen.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Someone used a rude nickname or word

Chainz filters common rude words, and you only ever play people you invite or who have your code. Tap ✕ to leave the room. To report something the filter missed, email us with the word and we'll add it.

Is my data private?

Yes. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. See the privacy policy.
