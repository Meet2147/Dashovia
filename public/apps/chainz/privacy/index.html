Privacy Policy

Chainz for iPhone · Effective 14 November 2026

In short: Chainz has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Online duels pass through our small game server, which keeps only the nickname you typed, a random ID and the two chains for the current round, and deletes the room within 3 hours of the last move. We don't sell or share anything.

Couch Duel

Couch Duel runs entirely on your iPhone. Nothing about it is sent anywhere.

1v1 Online

To play with someone on their own phone, both phones connect to the Chainz game server (run by Dashovia on Cloudflare). For each room it holds:

  • The nickname you typed. It doesn't have to be your real name, and your opponent sees it.
  • A random player ID created by the app on your phone, so you can reconnect if your signal drops. It isn't tied to your Apple ID, phone number or email.
  • The two chains for the current round and the race progress (words cracked, misses, hints, times). Your opponent only sees your hidden words after the race ends.

Each room deletes itself after 3 hours without activity. Leaving a room removes you from it straight away. The server keeps no request logs and no history of past games. Rude words are filtered from nicknames and chains.

Invite links

When you tap Send invite, your iPhone's share sheet sends a link with the 5-character room code through whatever app you choose. We don't see who you send it to.

Purchases

Chainz Pro subscriptions and the Lifetime unlock are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your Apple ID has an active purchase.

On your phone

Chainz stores your nickname, your random player ID and how many free duels you've started today. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up or account with us.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Chainz is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy or need support? Email contact@dashovia.app or see the support page.
