Privacy Policy

Brahma Muhurat for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch · Effective 15 October 2026

In short: Brahma Muhurat has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. Your location, alarms, japa counts, practice history and Health data stay on your devices. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

Location

Brahma Muhurat is measured backwards from your local sunrise, so the app asks for your location while you use it. Sunrise, Brahma Muhurat, the panchang and every alarm time are calculated on your device. Your coordinates are not sent to us.

To show the name of your city, the app uses Apple's geocoding service, which receives your approximate coordinates and is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. If you deny location access, the app uses a default city (Mumbai) and keeps working.

Apple Health (optional)

  • Sleep: if you allow it, the app reads last night's sleep from Health to tell you whether you rested enough before Brahma Muhurat.
  • Mindful Minutes: if you turn it on, each Dhyana timer sitting is saved to Health as Mindful Minutes.

Health data is read and written on your device only. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties. You can change these permissions at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

Alarms and notifications

Alarms and reminders are scheduled on your device using Apple's alarm and notification systems. Nothing about them leaves your device.

Your practice data

Your alarms, japa counts, mala choice, sankalpa vows, Dhyana sittings, morning check-ins and streaks are stored on your device and shared only with the app's own widget and Apple Watch app. They are not uploaded or backed up by us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Brahma Muhurat Pro subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple on your device to unlock Pro, and to recognise customers who bought the app before it became free.

Subscriptions are governed by Apple's standard Terms of Use (EULA). They renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and you can manage or cancel them in your Apple Account settings.

What we don't do

  • No sign-in or account.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
