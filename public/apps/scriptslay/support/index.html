Support

Scriptslay for iPhone

Need help? Email contact@dashovia.app with your device model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within a day or two.

Questions

How do I record with my script on screen?

Open a script and tap Record. Your script scrolls in the band at the top, right under the camera, so your eyes stay near the lens. Tap the text to pause, drag it to jump around, and use the 🐢 / 🐇 buttons to change speed. Drag the handle under the band to make it bigger or smaller.

It says the camera or microphone is off

Open iPhone Settings › Scriptslay and turn on Camera and Microphone. Without them you can still rehearse with the prompter.

What is Slay Sync?

A Pro feature: the script scrolls as you talk and waits when you pause. Turn it on in the recorder's Aa menu or in Settings, and allow Speech Recognition when asked.

Can I use it with a teleprompter rig or an external camera?

Yes. Open a script and tap Prompter for a full-screen prompter without the camera. Turn on mirror text (and flip if needed) for beam-splitter glass. Bluetooth keyboards and page-turner remotes work too: space plays or pauses, the up and down arrows change speed, and return starts or stops recording.

Where are my videos?

In the Takes tab. Tap a take to play, save it to Photos or share it. You can also turn on auto-save to Photos in Settings.

What's free, and what does Pro add?

Free includes 3 scripts, takes up to 60 seconds in 1080p and 3 templates. Pro unlocks unlimited scripts and take length, Slay Sync, 4K and 60fps, and every template. Choose yearly (with a 7-day free trial), monthly or a one-time Lifetime unlock.

Free trial, cancelling and refunds

  • Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you won't be charged.
  • Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
  • Changed phones or reinstalled? Tap Restore on the paywall or in Settings.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is there a Mac version?

It's coming soon. Tap the Mac card in the app to be told when it's out.

Is my data private?

Yes. There's no account, and your scripts and videos stay on your device. See the privacy policy.
