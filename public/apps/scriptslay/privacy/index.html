Privacy Policy

Scriptslay for iPhone · Effective 11 November 2026

In short: Scriptslay has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your scripts and the videos you record are saved only on your device. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

What the app stores

  • Your scripts: the titles and text you write, paste or import, plus your prompter and camera settings.
  • Your takes: the videos you record with Scriptslay.

All of this is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is not uploaded to any server of ours.

Camera and microphone

Scriptslay uses the camera and microphone only while the recorder is open, to record your video and its sound. Recordings are saved inside the app. They are copied to your Photos library only when you tap Save or turn on auto-save, and shared only when you use the Share button.

Slay Sync (speech recognition)

If you turn on Slay Sync, the app listens while you record so the script can follow your voice. It uses Apple's speech recognition. On devices that support it, this runs entirely on your device. On devices that don't, Apple may process the audio to turn it into text, under Apple's privacy policy. The words heard are used only to move the script and are not saved or sent to us.

Purchases

Scriptslay Pro subscriptions and the Lifetime unlock are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your Apple ID has an active purchase so it can unlock Pro.

What we don't do

  • No sign-in or account.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Delete scripts and takes inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything. Videos you saved to Photos stay in Photos until you delete them there. We hold no copy, so there's nothing more for us to delete.

Children

Scriptslay is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy or need support? Email contact@dashovia.app or see the support page.
